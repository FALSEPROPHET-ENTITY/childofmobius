TALE OF THE FIRST MOBIAN

The First Mobian stood in the empty field Among tranquil skies and resplendent flowers of a now forgotten age with wilted blossoms. The Mobian asked LEER: "What do I do here?" LEER answered: "I entrust you with the up-bringing of peace among your kind, and to all other living species within this celestial body." The Mobian looked around them. Fields upon fields stretching out into every direction under the bright sun. He found it hard to believe how he could find anybody here. "How do I find any-one? These fields stretch on forever..." LEER stared down at them. "Patience is a virtue, through time, and through good deeds, you will find others, whom you will treat with kindness, too." LEER vanished into thin air, leaving the Mobian to himself. And through years, The Mobian followed what LEER advised him. Showing kindness to the animals gifted. Doing everything in his power to be reverred. But truth is, I made sure he didn't get far.
